Camera & Motion Access
Companion Scan needs your camera to see the room and motion sensors to track your position. If prompted, please allow both for the AR scanning pipeline to track correctly.
Sweep the Room Slowly
Walk around steadily. Moving too fast will degrade SLAM tracking and cause drift. The live point cloud will show you which areas have been covered.
Three Ways to Capture
Tap the Capture button to run VLM object detection, toggle Auto-capture to snap frames automatically as you walk, or Stream Keyframes to send posed video for 3D Gaussian Splat reconstruction.
Track Scan Quality Live
The real-time quality chip indicates when the scan is ready. We will show you clear status alerts and recommendations if low light, reflections, or sparse coverage are detected.
Finish & View in 3D
Export the scan GLB when finished. A "View in 3D" link will let you open the desktop editor to inspect, measure, edit, and reconstruct the spatial world.
