# 0000. ADR template

- **Status:** Template
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related issue:** #NNN

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, product, organizational? Why is a decision
needed now? Keep it factual; describe the situation, not the answer.

## Decision

The change we are making, stated in the active voice: "We will …". Include the
key constraints it must satisfy.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. Trade-offs accepted, follow-up work
created, and anything a future reader should watch for. Note what would cause us
to revisit this ADR.
