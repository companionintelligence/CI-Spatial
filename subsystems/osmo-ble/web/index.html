About
Reverse-engineered DUML protocol. Pairing and the frame codec are checked against a real captured frame. Gimbal speed-control axis order/signs were corrected and live-verified via the joystick on 2026-08-21 (see protocol/commands.mjs). Absolute angle got the same axis fix by inference, not yet independently live-tested. The "mode" buttons above are confirmed to be a portrait/landscape toggle, not a real lock/follow/fpv selector. Shutter/record intentionally not implemented.
